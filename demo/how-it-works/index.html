How this demo works

Try a small change to the sample expense app. You review the plan and decide whether to keep the result.

  1. Describe

    Ask for an appearance or expense-rule change. Try: “Make view receipt match the rest of the look and feel.”

  2. Review and run

    Choose Create plan, review it, then Approve plan & run. The agents build and check the result. Compare Candidate with Accepted.

  3. Accept

    Choose Accept change after the checks pass. Until then, the accepted app stays as it was.

Choose a mode

Guided

Live
Choose Load Gemma to run on your device. The first download is about 2 GB; use a compatible desktop browser.
Recorded
Review your last successful Live run saved in this browser.

Changes stay local to this sample app. It supports appearance and expense rules; no real payments are made.
